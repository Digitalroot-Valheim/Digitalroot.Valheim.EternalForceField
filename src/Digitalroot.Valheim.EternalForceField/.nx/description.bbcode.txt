Adds a bone totem to the hammer. It can be found under `Defense`, `Furniture` and `Misc`. Build a bone totem with a force field to protect your base. The force field repels enemy NPCs the same way the force field around the traders works.

This mod is intended for casual play and should be avoided for competitive servers.
